Analytics engineering turns cleaned data into **semantic**, reusable models that power dashboards, experiments, and decision-making.

## Own the semantic layer

Metrics should have one definition. Revenue, active users, and retention mean different things to different teams unless someone owns the model.

## Test the models people trust

Schema tests, uniqueness checks, and accepted-value tests catch issues before executives see a broken chart. Treat analytics code with the same seriousness as application code.

## Partner, don't just deliver

The best analytics engineers sit with stakeholders, challenge ambiguous requests, and ship models that answer the real question—not just the ticket title.
